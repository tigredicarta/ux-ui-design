# Scenario

- **Action:** "Pause my reminders." → the app needs one clear place to pause them.
- **Result:** "They restart when I am back." → the app needs to show "Paused until…" and when reminders resume.
- Card sorting with classmates put "Pause while away" in the Reminders group.
