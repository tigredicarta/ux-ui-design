# Vaso

Vaso is a mobile app for people who look after plants at home. It keeps plant details and reminds people when plants need care.

- **Audience:** people who keep a few plants at home.
- **Main task for this flow:** pause reminders while away.
- **Scenario:** "I am leaving for two weeks. A friend will look after the plants." The person wants to pause reminders and have them restart when they are back.
- **Files:** `research/scenario.md`, `concept/concept.md`, `architecture/app-map.md`.
- **Research note:** Vaso is a class example. Instead of interviews, `research/` holds the scenario and the card sorting done in Lesson 5.
- **Output (Lesson 6, made by the AI agent):** `output/flow/flow.md`, `output/flow/index.html`, `output/wireframes/index.html`.
- **Limits:** keep the map's sections; no login, payments or backend.
